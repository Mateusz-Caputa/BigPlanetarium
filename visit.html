Getting Here

Big Planetarium

Bristol

We are a 5-minute walk from Bristol Temple Meads station. Bus routes 1, 8, and 72 stop directly outside. Limited on-site parking is available; we recommend travelling by public transport where possible.

The entrance is step-free and clearly signed from the main road. A hearing loop is fitted in the dome and at the ticket desk.
