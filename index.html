What is the Milky Way?

The Milky Way is the galaxy that contains our Solar System. It is a barred spiral galaxy roughly 100,000 light-years across, made up of around 200 billion stars, vast clouds of gas and dust, and at its centre a supermassive black hole called Sagittarius A*. From a dark sky on Earth, the Milky Way appears as a faint band of light arching across the night sky — that band is our edge-on view of the galaxy's disc. We sit inside it, about two-thirds of the way out from the centre, on a smaller arm called the Orion Spur.
